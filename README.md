# Customer Shopping Behavior Analysis

## Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing customer shopping behavior to identify purchasing patterns, customer preferences, spending habits, and factors influencing purchase decisions.

The project follows an end-to-end data analytics workflow using **Python for data cleaning and transformation, PostgreSQL for SQL-based analysis, and Power BI for interactive data visualization**.

The goal is to transform raw customer data into meaningful business insights that support data-driven decision-making.

## Objectives

- Analyze customer purchasing behavior and spending patterns.
- Identify high-revenue customer segments.
- Understand the impact of discounts and subscriptions on purchases.
- Identify top-performing products and categories.
- Analyze purchasing trends across different age groups and genders.
- Present insights through an interactive Power BI dashboard.

## Dataset

The Customer Shopping Behavior dataset contains **3,900 customer records** with attributes such as:

- Customer ID, Age, and Gender
- Item Purchased and Product Category
- Purchase Amount and Location
- Review Rating
- Subscription Status
- Discount Applied and Promo Code Used
- Previous Purchases
- Payment Method
- Frequency of Purchases

## Project Workflow

### 1. Data Cleaning and Preparation (Python)

- Loaded and explored the customer dataset using Pandas.
- Checked missing values and duplicate records.
- Standardized column names.
- Handled missing review ratings.
- Created customer age groups.
- Transformed purchase frequency into numerical values.

### 2. Data Analysis (PostgreSQL)

Used SQL queries to investigate business questions, including:

- What is the total revenue generated by male and female customers?
- Which customers spend more than average despite receiving discounts?
- What are the top five products with the highest average review ratings?
- How does subscription status relate to customer spending?
- Which products have the highest discount usage?
- What are the top three most purchased products in each category?
- How many repeat buyers belong to each subscription group?
- How much revenue does each age group contribute?

**SQL concepts used:** SELECT, WHERE, GROUP BY, HAVING, ORDER BY, aggregate functions, subqueries, CASE statements, CTEs, and window functions.

### 3. Data Visualization (Power BI)

Developed a Power BI dashboard to explore customer shopping behavior and communicate analytical findings.

The dashboard provides a visual approach to understanding customer segments, purchase activity, and business performance.

## Business Insights

The analysis is designed to help businesses:

- Understand which customer segments generate more revenue.
- Identify popular products and purchasing preferences.
- Evaluate discount and subscription behavior.
- Understand customer loyalty through previous purchases.
- Support marketing, customer retention, and sales strategies.
